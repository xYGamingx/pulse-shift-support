Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play LOW4 offline?

Yes. LOW4 supports offline gameplay against computer-controlled opponents.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to play against computer-controlled opponents.

How does online multiplayer work?

Online multiplayer uses temporary game rooms to connect players. A network connection and an available game server are required.

What if online multiplayer is unavailable?

Online availability depends on the game server and network connectivity. Offline gameplay remains available.

How do I report a bug?

Send an email to y.gaming@icloud.com with a description of the issue.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LOW4 processes information when you use the game, its online multiplayer features or this support website.

1. Data Controller

LOW4 is published by Y.Gaming under the developer account of Chayma El Ouariachi.

Privacy contact: y.gaming@icloud.com

2. Offline Gameplay

LOW4 can be played offline without creating an account.

Local gameplay statistics and solo game progress may be stored on your device using Apple's local storage facilities, including UserDefaults.

These locally stored game records are not automatically uploaded to our multiplayer server.

3. Online Multiplayer Data

When you use online multiplayer, LOW4 processes information necessary to create and maintain multiplayer sessions.

This may include:

  • Player display names or nicknames.
  • Temporary connection identifiers.
  • Game room identifiers and join codes.
  • Public or private lobby settings.
  • Player and session assignments.
  • Game status and session timestamps.
  • Cards, game moves and scores exchanged during multiplayer gameplay.

Player nicknames may be visible to other players when browsing available public game rooms.

This information is used to provide the online multiplayer functionality, coordinate players and maintain sessions.

4. Temporary Server Storage

Multiplayer lobby data is stored temporarily in server memory while required for active sessions.

Lobby information is generally removed when the hosting player disconnects or when the session expires. Sessions may remain in temporary server memory for up to six hours.

The current multiplayer server does not implement a persistent database for these lobby records.

5. IP Addresses and Network Data

LOW4 uses tunnl.gg as a network tunnel service to connect players with the multiplayer server.

The tunnel provider may process visitor IP addresses and network information for connection management, security, rate limiting and abuse prevention.

According to the provider's published privacy policy, connection logs may be retained for up to 30 days, or longer during an abuse investigation. IP-based restrictions may be retained for the duration of a restriction.

More information: tunnl.gg Privacy Policy

6. Gameplay Security

LOW4 uses Apple's CryptoKit framework, including HPKE, to protect multiplayer gameplay data exchanged between players.

The game relay forwards the protected gameplay packets without decrypting their inner gameplay contents. Connection metadata may still be processed by network providers.

7. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of LOW4 does not integrate third-party advertising, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry or similar analytics SDKs.

Multiplayer information is not used by Y.Gaming for advertising or cross-app tracking.

If these practices change in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

8. Support Communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, your message and any information you choose to provide.

This information is used to respond to support requests and resolve issues. Support messages are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or applicable legal obligations.

9. Support Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

The website itself does not integrate advertising trackers, analytics scripts or user registration forms.

GitHub may process technical connection information when delivering the website. Please refer to GitHub's privacy documentation for additional details.

10. Data Sharing

LOW4 shares the information necessary for multiplayer functionality with participating players and the network services used to operate online sessions.

We do not sell player information to advertisers or data brokers.

11. Legal Basis

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, multiplayer session data is processed to provide the service requested by players under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Network security and abuse-prevention processing may be based on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Support communications are processed to respond to requests and, where applicable, pursue legitimate interests in operating and improving support.

12. International Data Processing

Some service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area.

Where applicable, such processing must comply with relevant data protection requirements.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Offline gameplay information is stored locally on your device and may be removed by deleting the app and its local data.

Multiplayer lobby data is temporary, as described above. Network-provider information is subject to the provider's applicable retention policies.

To request information or deletion of data under our control, contact y.gaming@icloud.com.

We will review requests in accordance with applicable law. Some temporary records may already have expired by the time a request is received.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain personal data, restrict processing or object to certain uses of your data.

You may stop using the optional online multiplayer functionality at any time.

You may also contact the competent data protection supervisory authority if you have a complaint.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when LOW4 introduces new features or changes how information is processed.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
